## 🔧 Prerequisites for Instagram Connection:

### 1. Instagram Business Account
- Personal Instagram account → Professional Account → Business
- Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account

### 2. Link Instagram to Facebook Page
- Instagram app → Settings → Account
- Linked Accounts → Facebook
- Connect to your Facebook Page

### 3. Verify Connection
- Facebook Page Settings → Instagram
- Should show "Connected"

## 📝 How It Works:

1. User clicks "Add Instagram"
2. System fetches Facebook account
3. Checks if Instagram is linked to Facebook Page
4. Fetches Instagram Business Account ID
5. Stores in database
6. Shows in dashboard
