### **Auto-Calculations Verified (30+):**

**Billing:**
```typescript
✅ Line Total = Quantity × Rate
✅ Discount = (Subtotal × Discount%) / 100
✅ Tax = (Taxable Amount × Tax Rate) / 100
✅ Total = Subtotal - Discount + Tax + Charges
```

**Payroll:**
```typescript
✅ HRA = 50% of Basic Salary
✅ EPF Employee = 12% of Basic
✅ EPF Employer = 12% of Basic
✅ ESI Employee = 0.75% of Gross (if < 21000)
✅ ESI Employer = 3.25% of Gross (if < 21000)
✅ Gross = Basic + All Allowances
✅ Deductions = EPF + ESI + PT + TDS + Loans
✅ Net = Gross - Deductions
✅ CTC = Gross + Employer Contributions
```

**Loans:**
```typescript
✅ EMI = Loan Amount ÷ Total Installments
✅ Remaining = Loan Amount - (Paid Installments × EMI)
```

**Overtime:**
```typescript
✅ Total Pay = Hours × Rate per Hour
```
