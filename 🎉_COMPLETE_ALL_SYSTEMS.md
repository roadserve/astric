## 💡 **AUTO-CALCULATIONS**

### **Billing System:**
- ✅ Line Total = Qty × Rate
- ✅ Discount Amount
- ✅ Tax Amount
- ✅ Subtotal
- ✅ Total Amount
- ✅ Balance Due

### **Payroll System:**
- ✅ HRA = 50% of Basic
- ✅ EPF Employee = 12% of Basic
- ✅ EPF Employer = 12% of Basic
- ✅ ESI Employee = 0.75% of Gross
- ✅ ESI Employer = 3.25% of Gross
- ✅ Gross Salary = Basic + Allowances
- ✅ Total Deductions = EPF + ESI + PT + TDS + Loans
- ✅ Net Salary = Gross - Deductions
- ✅ CTC = Gross + Employer Contributions
- ✅ EMI = Loan Amount ÷ Installments
- ✅ Overtime Pay = Hours × Rate
- ✅ Pro-rata Salary based on working days
- ✅ Loss of Pay calculation
