## 🔥 **Pro Tips for AI Banner Creation**

### **Best Prompts Include:**
- **Specific dimensions**: 8ft x 3ft
- **Exact text content**: All your text
- **Color codes**: #0066FF, #00CC66, #FF6600
- **Style description**: Professional, modern, clean
- **Target audience**: Small businesses, accountants
- **Key features**: Automation, AI, billing, WhatsApp

### **Common Mistakes to Avoid:**
- ❌ Too vague prompts
- ❌ Wrong dimensions
- ❌ Missing key information
- ❌ Poor color choices
- ❌ Unreadable text

### **Success Tips:**
- ✅ Be specific about content
- ✅ Mention exact colors
- ✅ Include all text elements
- ✅ Specify professional style
- ✅ Ask for high contrast
