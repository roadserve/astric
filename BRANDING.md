## 📖 **Long Description** (200 words)

Astric.ai is the all-in-one business automation platform designed for small and medium enterprises looking to streamline operations and boost productivity. Built with cutting-edge AI technology, Astric.ai enables businesses to automate repetitive tasks, manage customer relationships, and orchestrate complex workflows without writing a single line of code.

Our visual workflow builder features 60+ pre-built integrations including email, WhatsApp, SMS, databases, payment gateways, and AI services like OpenAI and Claude. Whether you're automating invoice generation, customer onboarding, daily reports, or marketing campaigns, Astric.ai makes it simple and intuitive.

Key features include:
- **Visual Workflow Builder** - Drag-and-drop interface with real-time data flow visualization
- **Smart CRM** - Manage customers, leads, and communications in one place
- **AI-Powered Automation** - Leverage AI for intelligent decision-making and content generation
- **Multi-Channel Communication** - Connect via WhatsApp, Email, SMS, and social media
- **Analytics & Insights** - Track performance with detailed analytics and reporting
- **Team Collaboration** - Work together with version control and commenting
- **Enterprise Security** - Bank-grade encryption and multi-tenant architecture

Perfect for e-commerce, consulting, marketing agencies, and service businesses. Start automating today and reclaim hours of your time.
