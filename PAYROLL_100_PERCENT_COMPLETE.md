### **✅ Auto-Calculations:**
- HRA = 50% of Basic
- EPF = 12% + 12%
- ESI = 0.75% + 3.25% (if gross < ₹21,000)
- Gross = Basic + Allowances
- Deductions = EPF + ESI + PT + TDS + Loans
- Net = Gross - Deductions
- CTC = Gross + Employer Contributions
- EMI = Loan Amount ÷ Installments
- Overtime = Hours × Rate
